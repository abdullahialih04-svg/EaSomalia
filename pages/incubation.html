How the programme works

We are building the first cohort now. This is the path we are designing, and dates and support will be confirmed soon.

1. Explore

Join discussions, meet your cohort and learn how to compare problems and ideas.

2. Choose and match

Pick a cause area and an evidence-backed idea, then find a co-founder.

3. Plan

Test your idea, shape your strategy and prepare a proposal.

4. Launch

Seek funding and start building, with support from mentors and the community.

Three cause areas

Animal welfare

Reduce the suffering of the very large number of animals in farming systems.

Global health

Cut illness and early death with low-cost, proven interventions.

AI safety and governance

Reduce the risks of advanced AI through policy, research and field-building.

Who it's for

There is no typical founder. You can be a student, doctor, engineer, lawyer, vet or organiser.

You don't need

  • Non-profit experience
  • A charity idea
  • A specific degree
  • All the answers on day one

You do need

  • A commitment to doing good
  • Ambition to tackle important problems
  • The ability to learn quickly
  • Persistence through challenges

Common questions

I have my own idea. Can I join?

Yes. Tell us about it when you register. We will look at the evidence behind it and how it fits one of our three cause areas.

Will you help me find a co-founder?

That is a core goal. Founding with a well-matched partner is usually stronger than founding alone.

Do I need to be in Somalia?

Details are being finalised. Tell us where you are based when you register.

Is it funded?

We are still working out funding and support for the first cohort. We will share details as soon as they are confirmed.
