Everything you need to launch a high-impact organisation

EA Somalia is developing an incubation programme to help founders identify evidence-backed opportunities, find co-founders, and build organisations that tackle important problems in Somalia. We welcome ideas across animal welfare, global health, AI safety and governance, and other neglected areas where effective action could make a substantial difference.

Build something that matters

Founding an organisation is challenging. It requires making decisions under uncertainty, learning new skills, and staying committed to a mission. It can also be an opportunity to address an important problem that receives too little attention.

Meaning

Work on a neglected problem where your efforts could make a meaningful difference.

Ownership

Turn an evidence-backed idea into a project or organisation built to create positive change.

Personal growth

Develop your leadership, research, strategy, and organisational skills while working on something important.

How the programme works

We are developing the first cohort. The following outlines the programme we are designing; dates, eligibility, and available support will be confirmed as plans develop.

1. Explore

Learn how to identify neglected problems, assess evidence, and compare potential opportunities to make a difference.

2. Choose and match

Identify a promising problem and project idea, then connect with potential co-founders who share your mission.

3. Plan

Investigate the problem, test your assumptions, develop a strategy, and prepare a plan for creating impact.

4. Launch

Explore funding opportunities, establish your project or organisation, and build a community around your mission.

Problems worth solving

We are particularly interested in neglected problems where evidence-backed action could improve many lives or substantially reduce suffering. These are examples, not an exhaustive list.

Animal welfare

Find practical ways to reduce animal suffering, improve farming practices, and advance animal welfare in Somalia.

Global health and development

Address preventable illness, early death, poverty, and other barriers to human flourishing through effective interventions.

AI safety and governance

Help ensure that AI systems are developed and used safely, fairly, and in ways that benefit Somali communities.

Have an idea outside these areas? We are also interested in promising projects addressing other important and neglected problems.

Who it's for

You might be a student, researcher, doctor, engineer, lawyer, veterinarian, community organiser, or someone with a different background. You do not need to have founded an organisation before. What matters is your willingness to learn, investigate problems carefully, and work towards making a positive difference.

You don't need

  • Previous non-profit experience
  • An established charity or project
  • A specific degree
  • All the answers on day one

You do need

  • A commitment to doing good
  • Ambition to tackle important problems
  • A willingness to learn and follow evidence
  • Persistence through challenges

This could be you

If you want to start a project or found a charity that tackles Somalia's most neglected problems and has the potential to improve the most lives, this is your place. You do not need to have everything figured out already. Start with a problem you care about, a willingness to follow the evidence, and the ambition to make a difference.
