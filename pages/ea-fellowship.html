EA Fellowship

Weeks of reading and small-group discussion on how to do the most good. You will learn to compare options, reason under uncertainty, and choose between problems, and finish with a concrete plan for your career and giving.

Apply now

Why this matters

Most of us want to help. The harder part is working out how to help well, because our instincts can mislead us and good intentions sometimes backfire.

The gap between an average approach and an excellent one can be huge: not 10% better, but many times better. Effective altruism is about finding those approaches and then acting on them.

What you will do

  1. Read and reflect. Short weekly readings on the world's most pressing problems.
  2. Discuss in a small group. Test ideas with peers and a facilitator.
  3. Plan your next step. Finish with a plan for your career, giving or projects.
