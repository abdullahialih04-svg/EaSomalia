What is an effective donation?

Effective giving is about giving wisely, not only giving. The aim is for your donation to do as much good as it possibly can.

Why does it matter?

Good intentions deserve great results. Charities differ enormously in what they achieve with each dollar, so it pays to choose ones backed by transparent evidence and independent, rigorous evaluation.

How do you choose?

Giving What We Can is a global community of donors who give effectively. It points you to evaluated charities and funds and helps you commit to giving over the long term.

Charities and funds to explore

These organisations are well known in effective giving. Read how each one works and decide what fits your values.

GiveWell

Researches charities and recommends those that save or improve the most lives per dollar.

Animal Charity Evaluators

Evaluates animal charities and recommends the ones that help the most animals.

FarmKind

A giving platform that makes it easy to support effective farmed animal charities.

GiveDirectly

Sends money directly to people living in poverty, so they decide what they need most.

Coefficient Giving

A philanthropic funder that supports high-impact work across several cause areas.

EA Funds

Donate to funds run by expert grantmakers who choose where the money goes.

Trust the evaluators

Independent research groups assess charities and publish their reasoning so you can check it. Good places to start are GiveWell, Animal Charity Evaluators, Giving Green and Founders Pledge.

Even the evaluators get evaluated. See how Giving What We Can reviews them.
