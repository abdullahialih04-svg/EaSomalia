What is effective altruism?

Effective altruism combines evidence, reason, and a commitment to helping others to work out how we can do the most good.

Some help goes much further

Different ways of helping can have very different results. We look for opportunities to make a greater positive difference.

Focus on neglected problems

We look for large problems where additional people, time, and resources can make a meaningful difference.

Think like a scientist

We follow the best available evidence, stay humble, and change course when we learn something new.

Work it out together

Nobody figures this out alone. We explore ideas, learn together, and turn promising insights into action.
