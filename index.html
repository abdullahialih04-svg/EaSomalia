ABOUT EFFECTIVE ALTRUISM

Doing the most good we can

Effective altruism combines evidence, reason, and a commitment to helping others to work out how we can do the most good.

At EA Somalia, we create opportunities to learn, discuss important questions, build connections, and explore ways to make a positive difference locally and globally.

Evidence first

Ask what works, examine the available evidence, and remain open to changing our minds.

Important problems

Explore problems that affect many lives or cause severe suffering, including problems that receive too little attention.

Collective learning

Learn alongside people who care about making a difference and think carefully about how to do so.
